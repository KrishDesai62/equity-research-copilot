# Sampled negative coverage screening — October 23, 2026

Completed automated screening of 1,328 primary SEC reports for all 60 sampled negative companies. All inventoried documents downloaded and parsed; no declared overlapping submission-history files were missing. 172 reports contain bankruptcy/petition/receivership terms and require contextual assessment. These are keyword flags, not findings that the issuer filed bankruptcy.

Open [the evidence packet](../data/processed/validation/sampled_negative_coverage_v1/review.html). Expand each company, review flagged sources, and assess the complete filing inventory. The packet includes source URLs, dates, statuses and excerpts; per-company JSON files preserve document hashes. The existing source checkpoints and final outcomes were not changed.

Scope: reports filed after December 31, 2024 through July 19, 2026; qualifying petitions must occur in 2025. Current, annual, quarterly and applicable foreign primary reports and amendments are included. Up to 12 flagged excerpts per document are displayed; open the full document for all context.

Limits: inventory comes from saved SEC submission evidence, not an independent reconciliation against every SEC index. Exhibits are not screened. No keyword match does not establish a negative. No human attestations, model scoring, label changes or production approvals were generated.

Validation: five focused tests passed, covering reproducible sampling, missing/changed evidence, missing history declarations and blocked report text.
